About Heritage Atlas

Learn about this educational student project and its mission to make world heritage easier to explore.

Who we are

A small project with a big curiosity about history.

Heritage Atlas is an educational student project created to introduce visitors to notable historical monuments and heritage places around the world. It brings together short introductions, photographs and downloadable learning material in one place.

Our purpose

Make the past easier to explore.

We want students, travellers and curious readers to discover the people, cultures and ideas behind remarkable places—and understand why protecting heritage matters.

What you can explore

Four regions. Many stories.

  • North, South, East and West zone collections
  • Monument descriptions and visual galleries
  • Selected downloadable PDF guides
  • Contact and feedback pages for this project
Our vision

Understand yesterday. Value tomorrow.

Historical places connect us with earlier civilizations and shared human creativity. Our vision is to encourage learning, curiosity and respect for cultural heritage among future generations.
